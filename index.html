8 IDIOMS
01

make a clean breast of something

UKINFORMAL

坦白承认错误或秘密

  1. He decided to make a clean breast of the mistake before the audit began.

    审计开始前，他决定坦白承认这个错误。

  2. Making a clean breast of the problem early saved the team more trouble later.

    及早坦白问题让团队避免了日后更多麻烦。

02

be dead in the water

USINFORMAL

陷入停滞，无法推进

  1. Without legal approval, the expansion plan is dead in the water.

    没有法务批准，扩张计划将完全无法推进。

  2. The proposal was dead in the water once the funding was withdrawn.

    资金被撤回后，这项提案就陷入了停滞。

03

put a damper on something

UK / USINFORMAL

扫兴；削弱热情或进展

  1. The delay put a damper on the team's excitement about the launch.

    延期削弱了团队对发布的兴奋感。

  2. Rising costs have put a damper on plans to expand the office.

    成本上升给扩建办公室的计划泼了冷水。

04

go out of your way

UK / USNEUTRAL

特地付出额外努力帮助某人或完成某事

  1. She went out of her way to make the new colleagues feel welcome.

    她特地付出额外努力，让新同事感到受欢迎。

  2. The supplier went out of its way to resolve the delivery issue quickly.

    供应商特地加快处理，以解决交付问题。

05

keep someone at arm's length

UK / USNEUTRAL

与某人保持距离；不让其过度介入

  1. The company kept the consultant at arm's length from confidential discussions.

    公司让顾问与机密讨论保持一定距离。

  2. She keeps difficult clients at arm's length while remaining professional.

    她在保持专业的同时，与难相处的客户保持距离。

06

a long shot

UK / USINFORMAL

成功可能性很小的尝试

  1. Winning the contract is a long shot, but the proposal is worth submitting.

    赢得这份合同希望不大，但这份提案仍值得提交。

  2. It is a long shot that the team can finish by Friday.

    团队能在周五前完成的可能性很小。

07

hold your ground

UK / USNEUTRAL

坚持立场，不轻易让步

  1. The designer held her ground when asked to remove key accessibility features.

    被要求删除关键无障碍功能时，这位设计师坚持了自己的立场。

  2. You should hold your ground if the requested change creates a safety risk.

    如果所要求的变更会带来安全风险，你应坚持立场。

08

not miss a beat

USINFORMAL

毫不停顿地继续；立即适应

  1. After the handover, the new manager did not miss a beat.

    交接后，新经理立刻适应了工作，没有丝毫停顿。

  2. The team did not miss a beat when the meeting moved online.

    会议转到线上后，团队立即适应，没有受到影响。

02

take the rough with the smooth

UKNEUTRAL

  1. Running a business means taking the rough with the smooth.

    经营企业意味着既要享受顺境，也要承受逆境。

  2. She takes the rough with the smooth and rarely complains about setbacks.

    她坦然面对顺境与逆境，很少抱怨挫折。

03

make waves

UK / USINFORMAL

  1. The new pricing model is already making waves in the industry.

    新的定价模式已经在行业内引起了强烈反响。

  2. He avoided making waves during his first month in the role.

    任职第一个月，他避免做出打破现状的举动。

04

UK / USINFORMAL

  1. Despite a difficult start, the smaller team came out on top.

    尽管开局艰难，规模较小的团队最终取得了胜利。

  2. Companies that adapt quickly are likely to come out on top.

05

keep the lid on something

UK / USINFORMAL

控制局势；防止消息或问题扩散

  1. Management tried to keep the lid on the dispute until the investigation ended.

  2. Clear communication helped keep the lid on customer anxiety.

    清晰的沟通有助于控制客户的焦虑情绪。

06

UK / USNEUTRAL

  1. The current contract will run its course before the new terms take effect.

  2. We decided to let the discussion run its course before making a decision.

    我们决定让讨论自然进行完毕后再作决定。

07

USINFORMAL

  1. The old dashboard does not hold a candle to the new one.

    旧仪表盘远远比不上新的。

  2. No online tutorial can hold a candle to learning from an experienced mentor.

    任何在线教程都远不及向经验丰富的导师学习。

08

pull the plug

UK / USINFORMAL

终止项目、计划或支持

  1. The board pulled the plug on the project after costs doubled.

    成本翻倍后，董事会终止了该项目。

  2. We should fix the main issue before deciding to pull the plug.

    在决定终止计划之前，我们应该先解决主要问题。
