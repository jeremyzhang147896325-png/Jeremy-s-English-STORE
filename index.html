01

keep your finger on the pulse

UK / USNEUTRAL

持续掌握最新动态

  1. Good product managers keep their finger on the pulse of customer needs.

    优秀的产品经理会持续掌握客户需求的最新变化。

  2. The weekly report helps us keep our finger on the pulse of the market.

    每周报告帮助我们持续了解市场动态。

02

cross that bridge when you come to it

UK / USINFORMAL

等问题真正出现时再处理

  1. We may need more staff later, but we will cross that bridge when we come to it.

    以后我们可能需要更多员工，但等问题真正出现时再处理。

  2. Do not worry about the renewal yet; we can cross that bridge when we come to it.

    现在先别担心续约，等到时候再处理。

03

move heaven and earth

UK / USINFORMAL

竭尽全力做某事

  1. The support team moved heaven and earth to restore the client's data.

    支持团队竭尽全力恢复了客户的数据。

  2. We will move heaven and earth to meet the regulatory deadline.

    我们会竭尽全力赶上监管截止日期。

04

have a bone to pick with someone

UK / USINFORMAL

有问题要找某人理论

  1. I have a bone to pick with the vendor about the unexplained charge.

    我得就那笔不明收费找供应商理论。

  2. She had a bone to pick with me over the changes to the schedule.

    她要就日程变更的问题找我理论。

05

leave someone in the lurch

UK / USINFORMAL

在困难时丢下某人不管

  1. The contractor left us in the lurch by quitting halfway through the job.

    承包商在工作进行到一半时退出，把我们丢在了困境中。

  2. I promised to cover her shift, so I cannot leave her in the lurch now.

    我答应替她顶班，所以现在不能丢下她不管。

06

put your money where your mouth is

UK / USINFORMAL

用实际行动证明自己的话

  1. If management supports training, it should put its money where its mouth is.

    如果管理层支持培训，就应该用实际投入证明这一点。

  2. He put his money where his mouth was and invested in the project himself.

    他用实际行动兑现承诺，亲自投资了这个项目。

07

a sticking point

UK / USNEUTRAL

阻碍达成协议的关键争议点

  1. Ownership of the data remains the main sticking point in the negotiation.

    数据所有权仍是谈判中的主要争议点。

  2. The salary was acceptable, but remote work became a sticking point.

    薪资可以接受，但远程办公成了关键分歧。

08

take the heat

USINFORMAL

承受批评、压力或责任

  1. The project lead took the heat when the launch was delayed.

    发布延期后，项目负责人承受了批评。

  2. She was willing to take the heat for a decision she believed was right.

    她愿意为自己认为正确的决定承担压力。

    较小的公司率先推出更简单的产品，从而扭转了局势。

    一场有力的第二次演示可能会使局势转而对我们有利。

02

back to square one

回到起点；从头再来

  2. The failed test sent the engineering team back to square one.

    测试失败使工程团队不得不从头再来。

  2. Working without proper breaks eventually took a toll on his health.
