8 IDIOMS
01

go pear-shaped

UKINFORMAL

出岔子；变得一团糟

  1. The launch went pear-shaped when the payment system stopped working.

    支付系统停止工作后，发布活动出了岔子。

  2. Our travel plans went pear-shaped after the last train was cancelled.

    末班火车取消后，我们的旅行计划变得一团糟。

02

throw a spanner in the works

UKINFORMAL

给计划制造麻烦；横生枝节

  1. The unexpected audit threw a spanner in the works for the expansion schedule.

    突如其来的审计给扩张计划的时间表制造了麻烦。

  2. A supplier's delay threw a spanner in the works, but we found a workaround.

    供应商的延误打乱了计划，但我们找到了替代办法。

03

raise the bar

UK / USNEUTRAL

提高标准或要求

  1. The new accessibility guidelines have raised the bar for every product team.

    新的无障碍指南提高了所有产品团队的标准。

  2. Her presentation raised the bar for the rest of the conference.

    她的演讲提高了本次会议后续内容的标准。

04

the ball is in your court

UK / USNEUTRAL

轮到你采取行动或作决定了

  1. We have sent the revised contract, so the ball is in your court now.

    我们已经发出了修订后的合同，现在轮到你采取行动了。

  2. I have explained the risks; the ball is in the board's court.

    我已经解释了风险，现在轮到董事会作决定了。

05

under the microscope

UK / USNEUTRAL

受到严密审查或密切关注

  1. The department has been under the microscope since the budget overrun.

    预算超支后，该部门一直受到严密审查。

  2. Any changes to the safety process will be under the microscope.

    对安全流程的任何改动都会受到密切关注。

06

weather the storm

UK / USNEUTRAL

经受住困难时期

  1. The company reduced costs carefully to weather the storm.

    公司谨慎削减成本，以度过这段困难时期。

  2. With steady leadership, the team managed to weather the storm.

    在稳定的领导下，团队设法经受住了这场困难。

07

on the back burner

USINFORMAL

暂时搁置；放到次要位置

  1. We have put the redesign on the back burner until the funding is confirmed.

    在资金确认之前，我们暂时搁置了重新设计的计划。

  2. The training initiative was placed on the back burner during the merger.

    合并期间，培训计划被暂时放到了次要位置。

08

in the pipeline

UK / USNEUTRAL

正在筹备或开发中

  1. Several improvements are already in the pipeline for the next release.

    下一版本已经在筹备几项改进。

  2. The agency has three promising candidates in the pipeline.

    这家机构正在推进三名有希望的候选人。

8 IDIOMS
01

make a clean breast of something

UKINFORMAL

坦白承认错误或秘密

  1. He decided to make a clean breast of the mistake before the audit began.

    审计开始前，他决定坦白承认这个错误。

  2. Making a clean breast of the problem early saved the team more trouble later.

    及早坦白问题让团队避免了日后更多麻烦。

02

be dead in the water

USINFORMAL

陷入停滞，无法推进

  1. Without legal approval, the expansion plan is dead in the water.

    没有法务批准，扩张计划将完全无法推进。

  2. The proposal was dead in the water once the funding was withdrawn.

    资金被撤回后，这项提案就陷入了停滞。

03

put a damper on something

UK / USINFORMAL

扫兴；削弱热情或进展

  1. The delay put a damper on the team's excitement about the launch.

    延期削弱了团队对发布的兴奋感。

  2. Rising costs have put a damper on plans to expand the office.

    成本上升给扩建办公室的计划泼了冷水。

04

go out of your way

UK / USNEUTRAL

特地付出额外努力帮助某人或完成某事

  1. She went out of her way to make the new colleagues feel welcome.

    她特地付出额外努力，让新同事感到受欢迎。

  2. The supplier went out of its way to resolve the delivery issue quickly.

    供应商特地加快处理，以解决交付问题。

05

keep someone at arm's length

UK / USNEUTRAL

与某人保持距离；不让其过度介入

  1. The company kept the consultant at arm's length from confidential discussions.

    公司让顾问与机密讨论保持一定距离。

  2. She keeps difficult clients at arm's length while remaining professional.

    她在保持专业的同时，与难相处的客户保持距离。

06

a long shot

UK / USINFORMAL

成功可能性很小的尝试

  1. Winning the contract is a long shot, but the proposal is worth submitting.

    赢得这份合同希望不大，但这份提案仍值得提交。

  2. It is a long shot that the team can finish by Friday.

    团队能在周五前完成的可能性很小。

07

hold your ground

UK / USNEUTRAL

坚持立场，不轻易让步

  1. The designer held her ground when asked to remove key accessibility features.

    被要求删除关键无障碍功能时，这位设计师坚持了自己的立场。

  2. You should hold your ground if the requested change creates a safety risk.

    如果所要求的变更会带来安全风险，你应坚持立场。

08

not miss a beat

USINFORMAL

毫不停顿地继续；立即适应

  1. After the handover, the new manager did not miss a beat.

    交接后，新经理立刻适应了工作，没有丝毫停顿。

  2. The team did not miss a beat when the meeting moved online.

    会议转到线上后，团队立即适应，没有受到影响。
