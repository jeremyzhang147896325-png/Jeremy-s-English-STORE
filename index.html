8 IDIOMS
01

get your act together

USINFORMAL

组织好自己；恢复条理和效率

  1. We need to get our act together before the client review next week.

    我们需要在下周客户评审前把一切安排妥当。

  2. Once the team got its act together, the project moved quickly.

    团队恢复条理后，项目推进得很快。

02

keep your cards close to your chest

UK / USINFORMAL

对自己的计划或信息守口如瓶

  1. The company is keeping its cards close to its chest about the acquisition.

    公司对收购计划守口如瓶。

  2. He kept his cards close to his chest until the negotiations were almost over.

    直到谈判接近结束，他一直没有透露自己的底牌。

03

a blessing in disguise

UK / USNEUTRAL

表面上的坏事最终带来好结果；因祸得福

  1. Losing that account was a blessing in disguise because it freed us to pursue better clients.

    失去那个客户反而因祸得福，让我们能争取更好的客户。

  2. The delayed launch was a blessing in disguise; it gave us time to fix the main flaw.

    延期上线反而是件好事，让我们有时间修复主要缺陷。

04

read between the lines

UK / USNEUTRAL

从字里行间理解隐藏的意思

  1. If you read between the lines, the memo suggests that budgets will be reduced.

    如果读懂字里行间的意思，这份备忘录暗示预算将被削减。

  2. She can read between the lines and notices when a client is hesitant.

    她能理解言外之意，能察觉客户什么时候在犹豫。

05

a double-edged sword

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

双刃剑；既有好处也有风险的事物

  1. Remote work can be a double-edged sword for early-career employees.

    远程办公对职业生涯早期的员工来说可能是一把双刃剑。

  2. Publicity is a double-edged sword: it attracts customers but invites scrutiny.

    公开曝光是一把双刃剑，既能吸引客户，也会带来审查。

06

hold water

UK / USNEUTRAL

站得住脚；经得起推敲

  1. That explanation does not hold water once we compare it with the data.

    把它和数据对照后，这个解释就站不住脚了。

  2. The legal argument may hold water, but it needs stronger evidence.

    这个法律论点可能有道理，但需要更有力的证据。

07

go against the grain

UK / USNEUTRAL

违背某人的习惯、性格或主流做法

  1. It goes against the grain for her to reject a project without trying to improve it.

    不尝试改进就拒绝一个项目，这不符合她一贯的做事风格。

  2. The proposal goes against the grain of the organisation's usual approach.

    这项提案违背了该机构一贯的做法。

08

the writing is on the wall

UK / USINFORMAL

迹象已经很明显，坏结果可能即将发生

  1. The writing is on the wall for the old system now that support has ended.

    旧系统的结局已经很明显，因为技术支持已经终止。

  2. When three senior people resigned, the writing was on the wall.

    三位高层辞职后，事情不妙的迹象已经非常明显。

01

play devil's advocate

UK / USINFORMAL

故意提出反方观点，以检验某个想法是否站得住脚

  1. Let me play devil's advocate: what happens if demand falls after launch?

    让我故意唱个反调：如果上线后需求下降，会发生什么？

  2. She played devil's advocate to expose the weaknesses in our proposal.

    她故意从反方角度发问，以揭示我们方案中的薄弱之处。

02

fall through the cracks

USINFORMAL

因疏忽或制度漏洞而被遗漏

  1. Without a clear owner, small customer requests tend to fall through the cracks.

    如果没有明确负责人，客户的小需求往往会被遗漏。

  2. The new tracking system ensures that no application falls through the cracks.

    新的追踪系统确保任何申请都不会因疏忽而漏掉。

03

throw your weight behind something

UK / USNEUTRAL

利用自己的影响力全力支持某事

  1. The chief executive threw her weight behind the sustainability programme.

    首席执行官利用自己的影响力全力支持可持续发展计划。

  2. The proposal is likely to pass now that several senior leaders have thrown their weight behind it.

    几位高层领导已经表态支持，这项提案现在很可能会通过。

04

make a mountain out of a molehill

UK / USINFORMAL

小题大做；把小问题夸大成严重问题

  1. It was a minor formatting error, so let us not make a mountain out of a molehill.

    这只是一个小的格式错误，我们不要小题大做。

  2. He made a mountain out of a molehill when the meeting started five minutes late.

    会议只晚开始了五分钟，他却把事情说得非常严重。

05

a foregone conclusion

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

事先便可确定的结果；已成定局

  1. Approval is not a foregone conclusion, despite the strong initial support.

    尽管初步支持力度很大，获批仍不是板上钉钉的事。

  2. Once the rival bidder withdrew, the outcome seemed a foregone conclusion.

    竞争对手退出后，结果似乎已经没有悬念。

06

cut corners

UK / USINFORMAL

为省时间或成本而省略必要步骤、降低标准

  1. We cannot cut corners on security testing simply to meet the deadline.

    我们不能仅仅为了赶期限就在安全测试上偷工减料。

  2. The contractor cut corners, and the repairs had to be done again six months later.

    承包商偷工减料，导致维修工作六个月后不得不重做。

07

leave no stone unturned

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

想尽一切办法；彻底查找或调查

  1. The audit team will leave no stone unturned in its investigation.

    审计团队将在调查中查个水落石出。

  2. We left no stone unturned in our search for a suitable supplier.

    为了寻找合适的供应商，我们尝试了所有可能的办法。

08

go back to the drawing board

UK / USINFORMAL

原方案失败后重新规划；从头再来

  1. The prototype failed the safety test, so we have to go back to the drawing board.

    原型未通过安全测试，因此我们必须重新设计。

  2. After users rejected the new workflow, the team went back to the drawing board.

    用户不接受新流程后，团队回到起点重新规划。
