01

cut corners

UK / USINFORMAL

走捷径而降低质量或省略必要步骤

  1. Cutting corners on security testing can create serious problems later.

    在安全测试上走捷径，日后可能造成严重问题。

  2. The team refused to cut corners even with a tight deadline.

    即使截止日期很紧，团队也拒绝降低标准。

02

get the ball rolling

UK / USINFORMAL

开始推动某事；启动进程

  1. A short workshop helped get the ball rolling on the new strategy.

    一次简短的研讨会帮助启动了新战略。

  2. Could you get the ball rolling by introducing the first speaker?

    你能先介绍第一位发言人来启动流程吗？

03

take something with a pinch of salt

UKINFORMAL

对某事持保留态度；不要全信

  1. Take those early sales forecasts with a pinch of salt.

    对那些早期销售预测要持保留态度。

  2. I would take his claim of an instant fix with a pinch of salt.

    我不会完全相信他所说的能立即修复的问题。

04

read between the lines

UK / USNEUTRAL

体会字面意思之外的含义

  1. If you read between the lines, the email suggests that the deadline may move.

    如果体会一下言外之意，这封邮件暗示截止日期可能会调整。

  2. She read between the lines and realized the client was unhappy.

    她领会了言外之意，意识到客户并不满意。

05

keep your cards close to your chest

UKINFORMAL

不轻易透露自己的计划或想法

  1. The company is keeping its cards close to its chest before the announcement.

    在公告前，这家公司没有轻易透露自己的计划。

  2. He keeps his cards close to his chest during negotiations.

    谈判时，他不轻易暴露自己的想法。

06

go the extra mile

UK / USINFORMAL

付出额外努力，把事情做得更好

  1. The support team went the extra mile to help customers migrate their data.

    支持团队付出了额外努力，帮助客户迁移数据。

  2. Going the extra mile for a new colleague can make a real difference.

    为新同事多付出一点努力，确实能带来很大不同。

07

a tough row to hoe

USINFORMAL

艰巨而需要长期努力的任务

  1. Launching in a crowded market will be a tough row to hoe.

    在竞争激烈的市场中推出产品将是一项艰巨任务。

  2. They knew recovery would be a tough row to hoe after the outage.

    他们知道系统中断后恢复工作将非常艰难。

08

on the same page

UK / USINFORMAL

理解一致；意见一致

  1. Let's make sure everyone is on the same page before we contact the client.

    在联系客户前，先确保大家的理解一致。

  2. The design and engineering teams are finally on the same page about the scope.

    设计和工程团队终于就范围达成了一致。

    支付系统停止工作后，发布活动出了岔子。

02

throw a spanner in the works

UKINFORMAL

  1. The unexpected audit threw a spanner in the works for the expansion schedule.

    突如其来的审计给扩张计划的时间表制造了麻烦。

  2. A supplier's delay threw a spanner in the works, but we found a workaround.

    供应商的延误打乱了计划，但我们找到了替代办法。

03

  1. The new accessibility guidelines have raised the bar for every product team.

    新的无障碍指南提高了所有产品团队的标准。

  2. Her presentation raised the bar for the rest of the conference.

    她的演讲提高了本次会议后续内容的标准。

  2. I have explained the risks; the ball is in the board's court.

  2. With steady leadership, the team managed to weather the storm.

    在稳定的领导下，团队设法经受住了这场困难。

07

USINFORMAL

暂时搁置；放到次要位置

  1. We have put the redesign on the back burner until the funding is confirmed.

    在资金确认之前，我们暂时搁置了重新设计的计划。

  2. The training initiative was placed on the back burner during the merger.

    合并期间，培训计划被暂时放到了次要位置。

08

UK / USNEUTRAL

    下一版本已经在筹备几项改进。

  2. The agency has three promising candidates in the pipeline.

    这家机构正在推进三名有希望的候选人。
