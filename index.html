8 IDIOMS
01

turn a blind eye

UK / USNEUTRAL

故意视而不见；有意不理会

  1. The company cannot turn a blind eye to repeated safety concerns.

    公司不能对反复出现的安全问题视而不见。

  2. She turned a blind eye to the minor mistake because the deadline was close.

    因为截止日期临近，她对这个小错误没有追究。

02

get the wrong end of the stick

UKINFORMAL

误解情况或他人的意思

  1. I think you have got the wrong end of the stick; the meeting is next Tuesday.

    我想你误会了，会议是在下周二。

  2. He got the wrong end of the stick and assumed the offer had been withdrawn.

    他误解了情况，以为报价已经被撤回。

03

keep the wolf from the door

UKINFORMAL

勉强维持生计；避免陷入经济困境

  1. The freelance work was enough to keep the wolf from the door while she looked for a permanent job.

    在寻找固定工作期间，自由职业的收入足以让她勉强维持生计。

  2. The emergency loan kept the wolf from the door for a few months.

    这笔应急贷款让他们暂时避免了经济困境，撑过了几个月。

04

go the distance

USINFORMAL

坚持到底；完成长期或艰难的任务

  1. The project needs a leader who can go the distance.

    这个项目需要一位能坚持到底的领导者。

  2. Their early progress suggests the partnership can go the distance.

    他们早期的进展表明这段合作关系能够长期持续。

05

be a hard sell

UK / USINFORMAL

很难说服人接受或购买

  1. A price increase will be a hard sell unless we explain the added value.

    除非我们说明新增价值，否则涨价将很难被接受。

  2. Remote work was a hard sell to the board five years ago.

    五年前，要说服董事会接受远程办公非常困难。

06

hold your horses

USINFORMAL

别急；先等等

  1. Hold your horses; we still need legal approval before we announce anything.

    别急，在发布任何消息前我们还需要法务批准。

  2. Hold your horses and read the instructions before changing the settings.

    先等等，修改设置前请先阅读说明。

07

be a game changer

USINFORMAL

带来重大改变的事物或举措

  1. A reliable battery with twice the capacity could be a game changer for the product.

    容量翻倍的可靠电池可能会给这款产品带来重大改变。

  2. For small teams, automated reporting has been a game changer.

    对小团队来说，自动化报告带来了重大改变。

08

keep your powder dry

UK / USINFORMAL

保留实力；暂不行动以备后用

  1. The company is keeping its powder dry until market conditions become clearer.

    在市场形势更明朗之前，公司正在保留实力，暂不行动。

  2. Keep your powder dry rather than spending the entire budget in the first month.

    不要在第一个月就花光全部预算，要保留余力。

01

get cold feet

UK / USINFORMAL

临阵退缩；因紧张而犹豫

  1. She got cold feet just before signing the lease.

    她就在签租约前临阵退缩了。

  2. Do not let a difficult first week make you get cold feet about the new role.

    不要因为第一周很艰难，就对新职位打退堂鼓。

02

the last straw

UK / USINFORMAL

压垮人的最后一根稻草

  1. The delayed refund was the last straw, so he cancelled his membership.

    退款延迟成了压垮他的最后一根稻草，于是他取消了会员资格。

  2. For the team, another missed deadline was the last straw.

    对团队来说，又一次错过截止日期成了忍无可忍的最后一击。

03

see eye to eye

UK / USNEUTRAL

观点一致；达成共识

  1. The two founders do not always see eye to eye on pricing.

    两位创始人在定价问题上并不总是意见一致。

  2. We saw eye to eye on the goal, even though our methods were different.

    尽管方法不同，我们对目标的看法是一致的。

04

a mixed bag

UK / USINFORMAL

好坏参半；质量不一的组合

  1. The feedback was a mixed bag, with praise for the design but concerns about speed.

    反馈好坏参半：设计受到称赞，但速度引发担忧。

  2. The first round of applicants was a mixed bag.

    第一轮申请者的水平参差不齐。

05

level the playing field

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

创造更公平的竞争条件

  1. The grant is intended to level the playing field for smaller firms.

    这项补助旨在为较小企业创造更公平的竞争条件。

  2. Clearer rules could level the playing field between established brands and newcomers.

    更清晰的规则可以让成熟品牌和新进入者在更公平的条件下竞争。

06

sweep something under the rug

USINFORMAL

掩盖问题；对问题避而不谈

  1. We cannot sweep the customer complaints under the rug any longer.

    我们不能再掩盖客户投诉了。

  2. The review found that managers had swept several warning signs under the rug.

    审查发现管理人员曾掩盖若干预警信号。

07

put something on the back burner

USINFORMAL

暂时搁置，留待以后处理

  1. We have put the office redesign on the back burner until revenue improves.

    在收入改善之前，我们暂时搁置办公室改造。

  2. She put the side project on the back burner during the busy season.

    在繁忙季节，她暂时搁置了这个副项目。

08

be in the pipeline

UK / USNEUTRAL

正在筹备、开发或等待处理

  1. Several product updates are in the pipeline for the autumn.

    有几项产品更新正在筹备，计划于秋季推出。

  2. There are promising candidates in the pipeline, but no final decision yet.

    目前有一些有希望的候选人正在流程中，但尚未作出最终决定。
