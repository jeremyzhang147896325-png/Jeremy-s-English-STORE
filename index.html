8 IDIOMS
01

be up in the air

UK / USINFORMAL

尚未确定；悬而未决

  1. The venue is booked, but the final schedule is still up in the air.

    场地已经预订，但最终日程仍未确定。

  2. Our travel plans are up in the air until the client confirms the meeting.

    在客户确认会议之前，我们的出行计划仍悬而未决。

02

keep something at bay

UK / USNEUTRAL

阻止问题逼近或恶化；使其得到控制

  1. Regular maintenance helps keep serious system failures at bay.

    定期维护有助于防止严重的系统故障发生。

  2. The temporary funding kept the cash-flow crisis at bay.

    临时资金使现金流危机暂时得到控制。

03

have a change of heart

UK / USNEUTRAL

改变原来的想法或态度

  1. The director had a change of heart and approved the pilot project.

    主管改变了主意，批准了试点项目。

  2. She was ready to resign but had a change of heart after speaking with her manager.

    她原本准备辞职，但和经理谈过之后改变了想法。

04

take the plunge

UK / USINFORMAL

经过犹豫后勇敢采取重要行动

  1. After months of research, they took the plunge and launched the business.

    经过几个月的调研，他们终于下定决心创办了这家公司。

  2. I finally took the plunge and applied for the overseas position.

    我终于鼓起勇气申请了海外职位。

05

get to grips with

UKNEUTRAL

开始理解并有效处理复杂事物

  1. The new manager quickly got to grips with the team's main challenges.

    新经理很快理解并着手处理团队面临的主要挑战。

  2. It took me a few days to get to grips with the reporting system.

    我花了几天时间才掌握这套报告系统。

06

be at odds with

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

与某人或某事存在分歧或冲突

  1. The proposed deadline is at odds with the quality standards we agreed on.

    拟定的截止日期与我们商定的质量标准相冲突。

  2. The two departments have been at odds over how to allocate the budget.

    两个部门一直在预算分配方式上存在分歧。

07

lose sight of

UK / USNEUTRAL

忽视或忘记重要目标

  1. We should not lose sight of the customer while discussing technical details.

    讨论技术细节时，我们不应忽视客户。

  2. During rapid growth, the company lost sight of its original mission.

    在快速发展期间，公司忽视了最初的使命。

08

bring something home

UK / USNEUTRAL

使人深刻认识到某事的真实影响

  1. The customer interviews brought home how frustrating the old process was.

    客户访谈使我们深刻认识到旧流程有多么令人沮丧。

  2. Seeing the damaged equipment brought home the seriousness of the incident.

    看到受损的设备后，人们深刻认识到这起事件的严重性。

01

read the fine print

USINFORMAL

仔细阅读细则或合同小字条款

  1. Read the fine print before accepting the revised terms.

    接受修订后的条款前，请仔细阅读细则。

  2. The offer looked attractive until I read the fine print about the fees.

    这个优惠看起来很吸引人，直到我仔细看了有关费用的细则。

02

take something in stride

USNEUTRAL

泰然应对困难或批评

  1. She took the unexpected criticism in stride and improved the proposal.

    她泰然接受了意外的批评，并改进了提案。

  2. Good leaders take setbacks in stride without losing focus.

    优秀的领导者能泰然面对挫折，不会失去重点。

03

get a foot in the door

UK / USINFORMAL

获得初步机会，为进一步发展铺路

  1. The internship helped him get a foot in the door at a major firm.

    这份实习帮助他获得了进入大公司的初步机会。

  2. A small pilot project can get us a foot in the door with the client.

    一个小型试点项目可以帮助我们获得与客户进一步合作的机会。

04

raise eyebrows

UK / USNEUTRAL

引起惊讶、质疑或不赞同

  1. The unusually large expense raised eyebrows during the audit.

    这笔异常高额的支出在审计期间引起了质疑。

  2. His decision to leave without a backup plan raised a few eyebrows.

    他没有备用计划就离开的决定引起了一些惊讶。

05

on the same wavelength

UK / USINFORMAL

想法相近；彼此理解

  1. The design and engineering teams are finally on the same wavelength.

    设计团队和工程团队终于达成了默契。

  2. We were on the same wavelength about the kind of customer experience we wanted.

    我们对想要打造的客户体验有相同的理解。

06

in a nutshell

UK / USINFORMAL

简而言之；概括地说

  1. In a nutshell, the proposal reduces costs without lowering quality.

    简而言之，这份提案在不降低质量的情况下减少了成本。

  2. That is the situation in a nutshell: demand is strong, but supply is limited.

    概括地说，情况就是这样：需求旺盛，但供应有限。

07

by and large

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

总体上；大体而言

  1. By and large, the transition went more smoothly than expected.

    总体而言，这次过渡比预期更加顺利。

  2. The reviewers were by and large satisfied with the revised report.

    评审人员总体上对修订后的报告感到满意。

08

under the weather

UK / USINFORMAL

身体不适；感觉不舒服

  1. I am feeling a little under the weather, so I will join the meeting remotely.

    我感觉有点不舒服，所以会远程参加会议。

  2. She was under the weather for a few days but soon recovered.

    她身体不适了几天，但很快就恢复了。
