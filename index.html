8 IDIOMS
01

play devil's advocate

UK / USINFORMAL

故意提出反方观点，以检验某个想法是否站得住脚

  1. Let me play devil's advocate: what happens if demand falls after launch?

    让我故意唱个反调：如果上线后需求下降，会发生什么？

  2. She played devil's advocate to expose the weaknesses in our proposal.

    她故意从反方角度发问，以揭示我们方案中的薄弱之处。

02

fall through the cracks

USINFORMAL

因疏忽或制度漏洞而被遗漏

  1. Without a clear owner, small customer requests tend to fall through the cracks.

    如果没有明确负责人，客户的小需求往往会被遗漏。

  2. The new tracking system ensures that no application falls through the cracks.

    新的追踪系统确保任何申请都不会因疏忽而漏掉。

03

throw your weight behind something

UK / USNEUTRAL

利用自己的影响力全力支持某事

  1. The chief executive threw her weight behind the sustainability programme.

    首席执行官利用自己的影响力全力支持可持续发展计划。

  2. The proposal is likely to pass now that several senior leaders have thrown their weight behind it.

    几位高层领导已经表态支持，这项提案现在很可能会通过。

04

make a mountain out of a molehill

UK / USINFORMAL

小题大做；把小问题夸大成严重问题

  1. It was a minor formatting error, so let us not make a mountain out of a molehill.

    这只是一个小的格式错误，我们不要小题大做。

  2. He made a mountain out of a molehill when the meeting started five minutes late.

    会议只晚开始了五分钟，他却把事情说得非常严重。

05

a foregone conclusion

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

事先便可确定的结果；已成定局

  1. Approval is not a foregone conclusion, despite the strong initial support.

    尽管初步支持力度很大，获批仍不是板上钉钉的事。

  2. Once the rival bidder withdrew, the outcome seemed a foregone conclusion.

    竞争对手退出后，结果似乎已经没有悬念。

06

cut corners

UK / USINFORMAL

为省时间或成本而省略必要步骤、降低标准

  1. We cannot cut corners on security testing simply to meet the deadline.

    我们不能仅仅为了赶期限就在安全测试上偷工减料。

  2. The contractor cut corners, and the repairs had to be done again six months later.

    承包商偷工减料，导致维修工作六个月后不得不重做。

07

leave no stone unturned

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

想尽一切办法；彻底查找或调查

  1. The audit team will leave no stone unturned in its investigation.

    审计团队将在调查中查个水落石出。

  2. We left no stone unturned in our search for a suitable supplier.

    为了寻找合适的供应商，我们尝试了所有可能的办法。

08

go back to the drawing board

UK / USINFORMAL

原方案失败后重新规划；从头再来

  1. The prototype failed the safety test, so we have to go back to the drawing board.

    原型未通过安全测试，因此我们必须重新设计。

  2. After users rejected the new workflow, the team went back to the drawing board.

    用户不接受新流程后，团队回到起点重新规划。

01

move the goalposts

UK / USINFORMAL

临时改变规则或达标标准，使事情更难完成

  1. We had met every requirement, but the client moved the goalposts at the last minute.

    我们已满足所有要求，但客户在最后一刻改变了标准。

  2. It is frustrating when managers move the goalposts after the project has started.

    项目开始后，管理者又临时改变要求，这很令人沮丧。

02

read the room

USINFORMAL

察言观色；理解现场的气氛和他人的情绪

  1. Before making another joke, read the room and see how tense everyone is.

    再开玩笑之前先看看气氛，大家现在有多紧张。

  2. She reads the room well and knows when to raise a difficult topic.

    她很会察言观色，知道何时提出棘手的话题。

03

keep someone in the loop

UK / USINFORMAL

让某人持续了解进展；及时同步信息

  1. Please keep me in the loop while you negotiate the revised contract.

    你们协商修订后的合同时，请持续向我同步进展。

  2. The product team kept support in the loop about the launch date.

    产品团队让客服团队持续了解上线日期的进展。

04

a storm in a teacup

UKINFORMAL

小题大做；一场不必要的小风波

  1. The argument over the seating plan was really a storm in a teacup.

    围绕座位安排的争执其实只是小题大做。

  2. Once we checked the facts, the supposed crisis turned out to be a storm in a teacup.

    核实事实后，我们发现所谓的危机不过是一场小风波。

05

the elephant in the room

UK / USNEUTRAL

大家都知道却刻意回避的明显问题

  1. The budget shortfall was the elephant in the room throughout the meeting.

    预算缺口是整场会议中大家都回避不谈的明显问题。

  2. Someone should address the elephant in the room instead of pretending everything is fine.

    与其假装一切都好，不如有人直接面对那个明显的问题。

06

on the same page

UK / USINFORMAL

理解和目标一致；达成共识

  1. Let us meet briefly to make sure we are on the same page about priorities.

    我们简短开个会，确认大家对优先级的理解一致。

  2. The designers and engineers were finally on the same page about the user flow.

    设计师和工程师终于就用户流程达成了共识。

07

put the cart before the horse

UK / USINFORMAL

本末倒置；把步骤顺序弄反

  1. Hiring a sales team before validating demand would put the cart before the horse.

    在验证需求前就招聘销售团队，是本末倒置。

  2. We are putting the cart before the horse if we choose a vendor before defining the requirements.

    如果还没明确需求就先选供应商，我们就是把顺序弄反了。

08

call the shots

USINFORMAL

掌控局面；作最终决定

  1. The regional office calls the shots on pricing in that market.

    区域办公室负责决定那个市场的定价。

  2. She may not be the most senior person, but she clearly calls the shots on the project.

    她可能不是职位最高的人，但项目显然由她拍板。
