8 IDIOMS
01

have your hands full

UK / USINFORMAL

忙得不可开交；无暇兼顾更多事情

  1. I have my hands full with the product launch this week.

    这周我忙于产品发布，已经无暇兼顾其他事情。

  2. The support team has its hands full after the system update.

    系统更新后，支持团队忙得不可开交。

02

take the rough with the smooth

UKNEUTRAL

既接受顺境，也承受逆境

  1. Running a business means taking the rough with the smooth.

    经营企业意味着既要享受顺境，也要承受逆境。

  2. She takes the rough with the smooth and rarely complains about setbacks.

    她坦然面对顺境与逆境，很少抱怨挫折。

03

make waves

UK / USINFORMAL

引起重大反响；打破现状

  1. The new pricing model is already making waves in the industry.

    新的定价模式已经在行业内引起了强烈反响。

  2. He avoided making waves during his first month in the role.

    任职第一个月，他避免做出打破现状的举动。

04

come out on top

UK / USINFORMAL

最终获胜或取得优势

  1. Despite a difficult start, the smaller team came out on top.

    尽管开局艰难，规模较小的团队最终取得了胜利。

  2. Companies that adapt quickly are likely to come out on top.

    适应迅速的公司更有可能最终占据优势。

05

keep the lid on something

UK / USINFORMAL

控制局势；防止消息或问题扩散

  1. Management tried to keep the lid on the dispute until the investigation ended.

    管理层试图在调查结束前控制争议，不让其扩散。

  2. Clear communication helped keep the lid on customer anxiety.

    清晰的沟通有助于控制客户的焦虑情绪。

06

run its course

UK / USNEUTRAL

自然发展直至结束

  1. The current contract will run its course before the new terms take effect.

    现有合同将自然履行至结束，之后新条款才会生效。

  2. We decided to let the discussion run its course before making a decision.

    我们决定让讨论自然进行完毕后再作决定。

07

not hold a candle to

USINFORMAL

远远比不上某人或某事

  1. The old dashboard does not hold a candle to the new one.

    旧仪表盘远远比不上新的。

  2. No online tutorial can hold a candle to learning from an experienced mentor.

    任何在线教程都远不及向经验丰富的导师学习。

08

pull the plug

UK / USINFORMAL

终止项目、计划或支持

  1. The board pulled the plug on the project after costs doubled.

    成本翻倍后，董事会终止了该项目。

  2. We should fix the main issue before deciding to pull the plug.

    在决定终止计划之前，我们应该先解决主要问题。

    她愿意为自己认为正确的决定承担压力。
