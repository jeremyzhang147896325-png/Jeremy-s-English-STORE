01

miss the boat

UK / USINFORMAL

错过良机

  1. We missed the boat by waiting too long to respond to the tender.

    我们迟迟没有回应招标，错过了良机。

  2. Apply this week, or you may miss the boat.

    这周申请，否则你可能会错过机会。

02

have the upper hand

UK / USNEUTRAL

占据优势；处于有利地位

  1. With the latest market data, we have the upper hand in the negotiation.

    有了最新的市场数据，我们在谈判中占据优势。

  2. The supplier had the upper hand because there were few alternatives.

    因为替代选择很少，供应商占据了优势。

03

be on the fence

UK / USINFORMAL

犹豫不决；尚未表态

  1. Several board members are still on the fence about the expansion plan.

    几位董事会成员对扩张计划仍然犹豫不决。

  2. I was on the fence until I saw the full cost estimate.

    看到完整成本估算前，我一直没有决定。

04

get the ball rolling

UK / USINFORMAL

启动一件事；带头开始行动

  1. A short workshop should get the ball rolling on the new strategy.

    一场简短的研讨会应该能启动新战略的讨论。

  2. She got the ball rolling by introducing everyone to the client.

    她通过向客户介绍大家，带头推动了交流。

05

be in the driver's seat

USINFORMAL

掌握主动权；掌控局面

  1. After securing funding, the team is in the driver's seat.

    获得资金后，团队掌握了主动权。

  2. The buyer is in the driver's seat when demand is weak.

    需求疲软时，买方掌握主动权。

06

a grey area

UK / USNEUTRAL

界限不清、难以判断的领域

  1. Data ownership remains a grey area in the partnership agreement.

    数据所有权在合作协议中仍是界限不清的问题。

  2. The policy leaves a grey area around personal use of company devices.

    该政策对公司设备的个人使用留有模糊地带。

07

come to light

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

被发现；曝光；变得为人所知

  1. Several errors came to light during the final review.

    最终审查期间发现了几处错误。

  2. New evidence has come to light since the original decision.

    原决定作出后，出现了新的证据。

08

take something with a pinch of salt

UKINFORMAL

对某事持保留态度；不要全信

  1. Take the early sales forecasts with a pinch of salt.

    对早期销售预测要持保留态度。

  2. I would take his promise with a pinch of salt until we have it in writing.

    在拿到书面承诺前，我不会完全相信他的保证。

    我们需要在下周客户评审前把一切安排妥当。

02

keep your cards close to your chest

UK / USINFORMAL

对自己的计划或信息守口如瓶

  1. The company is keeping its cards close to its chest about the acquisition.

    公司对收购计划守口如瓶。

  2. He kept his cards close to his chest until the negotiations were almost over.

    直到谈判接近结束，他一直没有透露自己的底牌。

03

a blessing in disguise

UK / USNEUTRAL

表面上的坏事最终带来好结果；因祸得福

  1. Losing that account was a blessing in disguise because it freed us to pursue better clients.

    失去那个客户反而因祸得福，让我们能争取更好的客户。

  2. The delayed launch was a blessing in disguise; it gave us time to fix the main flaw.

    延期上线反而是件好事，让我们有时间修复主要缺陷。

04

read between the lines

UK / USNEUTRAL

从字里行间理解隐藏的意思

  1. If you read between the lines, the memo suggests that budgets will be reduced.

    如果读懂字里行间的意思，这份备忘录暗示预算将被削减。

  2. She can read between the lines and notices when a client is hesitant.

    她能理解言外之意，能察觉客户什么时候在犹豫。

05

a double-edged sword

UK / USFORMAL / NEUTRAL

双刃剑；既有好处也有风险的事物

  1. Remote work can be a double-edged sword for early-career employees.

    远程办公对职业生涯早期的员工来说可能是一把双刃剑。

  2. Publicity is a double-edged sword: it attracts customers but invites scrutiny.

    公开曝光是一把双刃剑，既能吸引客户，也会带来审查。

06

hold water

UK / USNEUTRAL

站得住脚；经得起推敲

  1. That explanation does not hold water once we compare it with the data.

    把它和数据对照后，这个解释就站不住脚了。

  2. The legal argument may hold water, but it needs stronger evidence.

    这个法律论点可能有道理，但需要更有力的证据。

07

go against the grain

UK / USNEUTRAL

违背某人的习惯、性格或主流做法

  1. It goes against the grain for her to reject a project without trying to improve it.

    不尝试改进就拒绝一个项目，这不符合她一贯的做事风格。

  2. The proposal goes against the grain of the organisation's usual approach.

    这项提案违背了该机构一贯的做法。

08

the writing is on the wall

UK / USINFORMAL

迹象已经很明显，坏结果可能即将发生

  1. The writing is on the wall for the old system now that support has ended.

    旧系统的结局已经很明显，因为技术支持已经终止。

  2. When three senior people resigned, the writing was on the wall.

    三位高层辞职后，事情不妙的迹象已经非常明显。
