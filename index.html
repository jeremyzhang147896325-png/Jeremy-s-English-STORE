8 IDIOMS
01

bite off more than you can chew

UK / USINFORMAL

承担超出自身能力的任务

  1. By accepting three major clients at once, the agency bit off more than it could chew.

    同时接下三家大客户后，这家代理公司承担了超出自身能力的任务。

  2. Be ambitious, but do not bite off more than you can chew in your first month.

    可以有雄心，但第一个月不要承担超出能力范围的工作。

02

pull strings

UK / USINFORMAL

利用人脉或影响力促成某事

  1. She pulled a few strings to arrange a meeting with the supplier.

    她利用一些人脉，安排了与供应商的会面。

  2. You should not have to pull strings to receive a fair review.

    你不应该需要动用关系，才能得到公平的评审。

03

take the edge off

UK / USINFORMAL

减轻不适、紧张或强度

  1. A short break took the edge off the tension before the presentation.

    演示前短暂休息缓解了紧张气氛。

  2. The discount should take the edge off the price increase for loyal customers.

    这项折扣应能减轻老客户对涨价的抵触。

04

hit a snag

USINFORMAL

遇到意外困难或障碍

  1. The launch hit a snag when the payment system failed its final test.

    支付系统在最终测试中失败，导致发布遇到意外障碍。

  2. Let me know as soon as you hit a snag with the application.

    如果申请过程中遇到任何难题，请立即告诉我。

05

put your foot down

UK / USINFORMAL

坚决表态并拒绝让步

  1. The manager put her foot down and refused to extend the deadline again.

    经理态度坚决，拒绝再次延长截止日期。

  2. At some point, you need to put your foot down about last-minute changes.

    对于临时改动，你终究需要坚决表明立场。

06

come full circle

UK / USNEUTRAL

兜了一圈又回到起点；形成完整循环

  1. After years abroad, her career has come full circle and she is teaching at her old school.

    在国外多年后，她的职业生涯兜了一圈，又回到母校任教。

  2. The discussion came full circle to the original question of customer trust.

    讨论最终又回到了最初关于客户信任的问题。

07

the tip of the iceberg

UK / USNEUTRAL

冰山一角；更大问题的一小部分

  1. The reported cases may be only the tip of the iceberg.

    已报告的案例可能只是冰山一角。

  2. These delays are the tip of the iceberg; the whole process needs attention.

    这些延误只是冰山一角，整个流程都需要关注。

08

a rule of thumb

UK / USNEUTRAL

经验法则；实用的粗略准则

  1. As a rule of thumb, allow an extra day for international deliveries.

    经验上，国际配送应额外预留一天。

  2. A useful rule of thumb is to test the simplest solution first.

    一条实用的经验法则是先测试最简单的方案。

8 IDIOMS
01

turn a blind eye

UK / USNEUTRAL

故意视而不见；有意不理会

  1. The company cannot turn a blind eye to repeated safety concerns.

    公司不能对反复出现的安全问题视而不见。

  2. She turned a blind eye to the minor mistake because the deadline was close.

    因为截止日期临近，她对这个小错误没有追究。

02

get the wrong end of the stick

UKINFORMAL

误解情况或他人的意思

  1. I think you have got the wrong end of the stick; the meeting is next Tuesday.

    我想你误会了，会议是在下周二。

  2. He got the wrong end of the stick and assumed the offer had been withdrawn.

    他误解了情况，以为报价已经被撤回。

03

keep the wolf from the door

UKINFORMAL

勉强维持生计；避免陷入经济困境

  1. The freelance work was enough to keep the wolf from the door while she looked for a permanent job.

    在寻找固定工作期间，自由职业的收入足以让她勉强维持生计。

  2. The emergency loan kept the wolf from the door for a few months.

    这笔应急贷款让他们暂时避免了经济困境，撑过了几个月。

04

go the distance

USINFORMAL

坚持到底；完成长期或艰难的任务

  1. The project needs a leader who can go the distance.

    这个项目需要一位能坚持到底的领导者。

  2. Their early progress suggests the partnership can go the distance.

    他们早期的进展表明这段合作关系能够长期持续。

05

be a hard sell

UK / USINFORMAL

很难说服人接受或购买

  1. A price increase will be a hard sell unless we explain the added value.

    除非我们说明新增价值，否则涨价将很难被接受。

  2. Remote work was a hard sell to the board five years ago.

    五年前，要说服董事会接受远程办公非常困难。

06

hold your horses

USINFORMAL

别急；先等等

  1. Hold your horses; we still need legal approval before we announce anything.

    别急，在发布任何消息前我们还需要法务批准。

  2. Hold your horses and read the instructions before changing the settings.

    先等等，修改设置前请先阅读说明。

07

be a game changer

USINFORMAL

带来重大改变的事物或举措

  1. A reliable battery with twice the capacity could be a game changer for the product.

    容量翻倍的可靠电池可能会给这款产品带来重大改变。

  2. For small teams, automated reporting has been a game changer.

    对小团队来说，自动化报告带来了重大改变。

08

keep your powder dry

UK / USINFORMAL

保留实力；暂不行动以备后用

  1. The company is keeping its powder dry until market conditions become clearer.

    在市场形势更明朗之前，公司正在保留实力，暂不行动。

  2. Keep your powder dry rather than spending the entire budget in the first month.

    不要在第一个月就花光全部预算，要保留余力。
